Before this Pinternship at VLED Lab, I had no prior knowledge of the MERN stack. Everything was new, and honestly, that was intimidating at first.

The learning on ViBe wasn’t easy. There was a lot of content, and the competition and peer pressure were very real. Watching others move fast sometimes made me question myself. But this experience slowly taught me something important—consistency matters more than speed. Instead of rushing, I focused on understanding.

There were days when things didn’t click immediately, especially during case studies. But by staying consistent and actually sitting with the problems, I was able to complete the course with clarity, not just completion.

This pinternship didn’t just teach me technical concepts. It changed how I handle pressure and learning. I learned to stay patient, trust my pace, and keep going even when comparison felt heavy.

That lesson is something I’ll carry forward.
